About Me

I’m from Ozamiz City, Misamis Occidental, and I was born on July 2, 2003. I’m someone who loves simple things that make life feel a little more beautiful—pink things, peaceful places, nature trips, and spending time near the water.

🌸

Hi, I'm Reshamei! ♡

I once dreamed of becoming a doctor, never imagining that nursing would become part of my story. I was shy and introverted, and the thought of constantly facing patients and speaking to people felt like a path I was never meant to take. Nursing was never my first choice—it was simply a road I found myself walking. Yet somewhere along the way, I began to fall in love with it. Perhaps nursing is my bittersweet love story—the kind that feels cruel and difficult, yet somehow keeps me coming back. Through sleepless nights, endless duties, heavy lessons, and moments of doubt, nursing slowly shaped me into someone braver than the person who first entered this journey. Now, I have come farther than I ever thought I could. I may not know exactly where this road will lead, but I know I want to see it through. And someday, I hope to wear the title I once never imagined for myself: Nurse.
